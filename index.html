06 · Questions

विचारायचं आहे?

How soon will I see results?

Consistent posting starts in the first month. Footfall and enquiries usually take two to three months of steady work. We show you the numbers every month, so you can judge for yourself.

Do you guarantee followers or sales?

No. Nobody honest can. We guarantee the work: a clear plan, regular posting and a monthly review with real numbers.

Do I need to spend money on ads?

Not at the start. Many businesses grow with content and creator collabs first. When ads make sense, we tell you, and the ad spend is separate from our fee.

Do I have to be on camera?

Only if you want to. Your staff, your food, your space or your customers can be the face of the page. We plan around what you are comfortable with.

Do you work outside Pune?

Yes, across Maharashtra. Shoots outside Pune are planned in advance.

Who owns my Instagram page?

You do. Always. We work on your page with your permission, and you can see everything we post.
